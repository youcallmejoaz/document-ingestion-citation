# Customer Service Standards

These standards apply to every channel and every team that talks to customers.

## Response times

- Emails: reply within 4 business hours. Business hours are 8:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.
- Phone: answer within 30 seconds. Missed calls are returned within 1 business hour.
- Live chat: first reply within 2 minutes.
- Social media messages: reply within 1 business day and move the conversation to email or phone if it involves account details.

If you can't solve the problem within the response time, send a holding reply that says what happens next and when the customer will hear back.

## Tone of voice

- Use the customer's name and sign off with your own first name.
- Write in plain English. Avoid internal jargon and system names.
- Apologise once, clearly, when we got something wrong. Don't blame other teams.

## Handling complaints

1. Acknowledge the complaint within 1 business hour, on the same channel the customer used.
2. Log it in the CRM as a Complaint case with the category and severity.
3. If you can resolve it within your approval limits, do so and confirm in writing.
4. If not, escalate to your team lead with a summary and the customer's preferred outcome.
5. Complaints open for more than 5 business days are escalated to the operations manager automatically.

## Data protection on calls

Before discussing an account, confirm the caller's identity with two of: full postcode, date of birth, last payment amount. Never read out full card or bank details.
