# HR Leave and Disciplinary Policy

Restricted: HR team and administrators.

## 1 Annual leave

Full-time employees get 25 days of annual leave a year plus public holidays. Part-time leave is calculated pro rata.

Book leave in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. Requests of more than 10 consecutive days need approval from the department head.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next leave year. They must be used by 31 March.

## 2 Sickness absence

Employees must tell their manager before 9:30 on the first day of absence. A fit note is required for absences longer than 7 calendar days.

## 3 Disciplinary procedure

1. The manager investigates and records the facts before any hearing.
2. The employee receives written notice of the hearing at least 48 hours in advance, including the allegations and the evidence.
3. The employee may bring a colleague or trade union representative to the hearing.
4. The outcome is confirmed in writing within 5 business days of the hearing.
5. The employee can appeal in writing within 7 calendar days of receiving the outcome.

## 4 Warnings

A first written warning stays on file for 6 months. A final written warning stays on file for 12 months.
