# New Starter Guide

Welcome to the team. This guide covers your first day, your first week and who to ask for help.

## Your first day

1. Collect your laptop and security pass from reception at 9:00.
2. Sign in with the temporary password in your welcome email and set up multi-factor authentication.
3. Meet your buddy, who will show you around and answer everyday questions for your first month.
4. Complete the Information Security module in the training portal before lunch.

## Your first week

1. Complete the mandatory training modules: Information Security, Data Protection and Health and Safety.
2. Read the Customer Service Standards and the Refund and Returns Policy.
3. Shadow at least two customer calls with your buddy.
4. Have your first one-to-one with your manager by Friday.
5. Check that you can access the CRM, the billing system and the shared drive. Raise an IT ticket if anything is missing.

## Systems you will use

The CRM holds customer records and cases. The billing system handles orders, invoices and refunds. The shared drive holds templates, contracts and policies. The training portal tracks your mandatory modules.

## Who to ask

- IT access problems: raise a ticket in the IT service desk portal.
- Pay and contracts: email the HR team.
- Day-to-day work questions: your buddy first, then your team lead.

## Probation

Probation lasts 3 months. Your manager will hold review meetings at 1 month and 3 months.
